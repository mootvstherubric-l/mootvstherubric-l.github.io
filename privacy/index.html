Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 June 2026

moot-vs-the-rubric (“we”, “the project”) is an educational, entertainment-oriented social-media experiment. This policy explains what we do and do not collect.

What we publish

We publish AI-generated educational content — mock-interview teardowns, coding patterns, and common pitfalls — to TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, X, and Substack. Scenarios are representative and do not contain any real company’s confidential interview questions or any individual’s personal information.

Information we collect

  • We do not run our own user accounts, logins, or databases of visitors. This site is a static informational page and collects no personal data, sets no tracking cookies, and runs no analytics.
  • Platform interactions. When you follow, comment, or send a direct message on a platform (e.g. Instagram), that interaction is handled by the platform under its privacy policy. We may read public comments and direct messages sent to us in order to respond. Instagram is our only monitored DM channel.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any personal information, because we do not collect it.

Platform APIs

We use the official posting APIs of TikTok, Meta (Instagram), YouTube (Google), and X solely to publish our own content and to read replies/DMs directed at our accounts. We store only the access tokens needed to post, in a private, non-public credentials file. We request the minimum scopes required to publish.

Children

The content is intended for working and aspiring software engineers and is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy: DM @mootvstherubric on Instagram.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above will change and the new version will be posted here.
